WiFi Setup

Join the car to a WiFi network. It keeps its own hotspot as a fallback, so if this network isn't in range next time, reconnect your phone to the car's hotspot to get back here.

Heads up: the Pi has one WiFi radio, so switching it to the new network drops your phone's connection to this page immediately - that's expected, not an error. Join your phone to the same network (or the car's hotspot if the join failed) and reopen this address to check the result.
